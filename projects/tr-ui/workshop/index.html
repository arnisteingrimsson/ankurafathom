What the checks establish

Engagements are conserved, employee time is exclusive, assignments respect skills, and revenue and costs reconcile to a separate employee/project ledger.

These checks test the implementation of our rules. They do not prove that the rules describe Ankura.

Where evidence can help

  • HR: workforce mix, skills and cost.
  • Utilization: delivery hours and availability.
  • Projects: effort, review, queues and completions.
  • CRM: arrival and win patterns.
  • AI pilots: task savings, rework and training response.
